This is the grassland gallery of the Animal Kingdom Grand Adventure Exhibition. Listen here to the zone guide, and look out over the widest open-air stage on Earth.

Play the gallery narration above. The transcript matches the audio.

草原就像是地表上最寬廣的露天舞台，上演著驚心動魄的動物大遷徙。

隨著乾濕季的週期更迭，雨水在不同區域交替降下，新生青草輪流生長，促使草食性動物們必須追逐這座移動的草場，而肉食性掠食者則緊隨其後，構成了震撼的「非洲大遷徙」。在這裡，除了動物以外，還有沈浸式投影，重現草原的日夜變化。

本展區將帶領大家，一起瞭望壯闊的草原生態，探索環環相扣的生命網絡。

The grassland is like the widest open-air stage on the surface of the Earth, where a heart-stopping great migration of animals plays out.

As the cycle of wet and dry seasons turns, rain falls in different regions in turn, and new green grass grows in rotation. Grazers must chase this moving pasture, and the meat-eaters follow close behind, making up the stunning Great Migration of Africa. Here, besides the animals, there is also immersive projection, bringing back the day and night of the grassland.

This gallery will lead you to look out over the vast grassland ecology, and to explore a web of life in which every ring holds the next.
