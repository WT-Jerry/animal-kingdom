This is a journey across hundreds of millions of years of Earth's ecology. From the modern world, we travel back to the primordial age when dinosaurs ruled. Six galleries—forest, desert, ocean, grassland, polar regions, and prehistoric life—trace the lineage of all life on land, in the sea, and in the air. Combining real animal specimens with immersive light-and-shadow experiences, each habitat is brought back to life, inviting visitors to walk through Earth's many beautiful corners and feel the diversity of life in a single visit.

Let us enter the Animal Kingdom Grand Adventure Exhibition, and begin this wondrous journey with the animals.
