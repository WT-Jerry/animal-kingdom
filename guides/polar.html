This is the polar gallery of the Animal Kingdom Grand Adventure Exhibition. Listen here to the zone guide, and step into the natural freezers at the two ends of the Earth.

Play the gallery narration above. The transcript matches the audio.

極地就像是地球兩端最神祕的天然冷凍庫，生物在一望無際的冰川與雪地間練就出一身抗寒本領。

在這片長年冰封的嚴峻環境中，生命面臨著全球最殘酷的低溫與失水考驗，動物們都演化出獨特的保溫模式，極地展區還原了高聳壯觀的冰川雪景，講述這群極端生存者如何在世界盡頭展現生命的堅韌。

本展區將帶領大家深入冰天雪地，一同探究極地居民們創造的生命奇蹟。

The poles are like the most mysterious natural freezers at the two ends of the Earth. In the endless glaciers and snow, life has trained a body made for the cold.

In this severe world, sealed in ice year after year, life meets the planet's cruelest tests of low temperature and lost water. The animals have all evolved their own ways to keep warm. This gallery restores the towering spectacle of glacier and snow, and tells how these extreme survivors show the toughness of life at the end of the world.

This gallery will lead you deep into ice and snow, to look into the miracles of life the polar residents have made.
