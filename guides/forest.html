森林就像是地球上最熱鬧的綠色公寓，擠滿了各式各樣不可思議的房客。

隨著地區環境不同，生物也發展出多變化的適應策略。在結構繁複的熱帶雨林中，高聳的植物創造了豐富的垂直空間，促使大量動物演化出高超的樹棲本領；而在四季分明的溫帶森林中，面對劇烈的季節溫差，地棲動物則充分利用身體恆溫優勢，透過冬眠、遷徙或換毛等行為來適應寒冬。

本展區將帶領觀眾一起跨越地理經緯，解鎖不同氣候帶下的生存秘密。

The forest is like the busiest green apartment on Earth, packed with all kinds of improbable tenants.

As the land changes, so do the strategies of the lives that share it. In the tangled tropical rainforest, towering plants open a rich vertical space, and many animals have evolved extraordinary gifts for life in the trees. In the temperate forest, where the four seasons are sharp, ground-dwellers make full use of a warm-blooded body, meeting the bitter winter through hibernation, migration, or a change of coat.

This gallery will lead you across latitudes and longitudes, unlocking the secrets of survival under different climates.
