海洋生態分區導覽

0:00 -0:00

這是《動物王國大探險展》的海洋生態分區。在此聆聽展區導覽，潛入這張覆蓋地球的藍色畫布。

上方可播放分區導覽語音。逐字稿與語音內容相同。

This is the ocean gallery of the Animal Kingdom Grand Adventure Exhibition. Listen here to the zone guide, and dive into the blue canvas that covers the Earth.

Play the gallery narration above. The transcript matches the audio.

海洋就像是一張覆蓋著大半個地球的藍色畫布，描繪著各種神秘美麗的故事。

在我們正式告別陸地、進入海洋展區之前，眼前的蟹類與螺類標本，就像是最後的海岸線，展現潮間帶豐富的生命足跡。穿越標本後，就是由360度光影打造的海中生態，從陽光灑落的珊瑚礁淺海，下沉到未知的黑暗深海，在這裡，生命不再受限於玻璃展櫃，而是重新活在光影編織中的奇幻世界。

本展區將帶領大家潛入充滿豐沛生命力的深海，一同悠遊迷人的海洋。

The ocean is like a blue canvas covering more than half the Earth, painted with all kinds of mysterious and beautiful stories.

Before we formally leave the land and enter the ocean gallery, the crab and snail specimens before you are like the last coastline, showing the rich footprints of life in the intertidal zone. After the specimens comes a sea ecology built of 360-degree light, from sunlit coral shallows down into the unknown dark of the deep. Here life is no longer bound by a glass case; it lives again in a fantastic world woven of light.

This gallery will lead you to dive into a deep sea full of abundant life, and to wander the enchanting ocean together.
